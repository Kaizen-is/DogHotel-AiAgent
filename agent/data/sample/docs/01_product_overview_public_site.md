# Product overview (from the public website)

Source: public pages of https://www.doghotel.com.ua/, read on 2026-10-16. Contact details are intentionally not copied.

## What the business is
A dog hotel in Kyiv, located in a private-house area, working for more than 30 years. It is one of the few dog hotels with veterinary support, and dogs are supervised around the clock. Large and "serious" dogs (e.g. rottweilers, Central Asian shepherds, akitas) are kept in separate summer and winter enclosures and walk separately to avoid fights. There is also a shelter part: dogs looking for a new home are shown in the "Want a friend" section.

## Public site sections (navigation)
- Home: intro, additional services, reviews, dogs looking for a home, latest blog articles, newsletter subscription
- Services & prices: hotel stay (including enclosure stays) and additional services
- About us: who we are, photo gallery, reviews, FAQ
- Want a friend (adoption): list of shelter dogs with filters by sex, age (under 1 year, 1-5, 5+) and size (small, medium, large); each dog has a profile page
- Blog: articles (e.g. "What to know before taking a dog from a shelter")
- Contacts: address and phone numbers; "Contact us" button in the header
- Footer: newsletter subscription form

## Additional services
Grooming, veterinary help, pet transportation.

## FAQ topics on the site
Home boarding vs hotel; whether dogs fight with other guests; 24/7 presence; vet for emergencies; feeding; how to find out the total price of a stay; flea and tick treatment; why vaccinations matter.

## Observations relevant to scope
- There is no online booking form today: customers contact the hotel by phone or the contact button.
- Adoption interest is also handled offline (no application form on dog profiles).
